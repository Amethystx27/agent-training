Call for Papers and Registration Information

The International Conference on Artificial Intelligence and Internet of Things (AIIoT 2027) will be held in Chengdu, China, from June 18 to June 20, 2027.

The conference aims to bring together researchers, engineers, and industry professionals to discuss recent advances in artificial intelligence, Internet of Things, edge computing, federated learning, and intelligent network systems.

1. Important Dates

Paper Submission Deadline: March 15, 2027
Acceptance Notification: April 20, 2027
Camera-ready Submission: May 10, 2027
Early Bird Registration Deadline: May 1, 2027
Conference Dates: June 18–20, 2027

2. Registration Fees

Participants are encouraged to complete their registration before the early bird deadline to enjoy discounted registration fees.

The registration fees are listed below:

Registration Category | Early Bird Fee (Before May 1) | Regular Fee (On/After May 1)
Regular Author | USD 400 | USD 500
Student Author | USD 280 | USD 350
Non-author Participant | USD 200 | USD 260

3. Registration Policy

The student registration rate is available only to participants who hold valid full-time student status.
Student authors must provide a valid student ID or an official enrollment certificate.
Each accepted paper must be covered by at least one full registration.
The early bird discount applies only to registrations with successful payment received before May 1, 2027.
Registration fees include access to all technical sessions, conference materials, coffee breaks, and lunches.
Accommodation and travel expenses are not included in the registration fees.
