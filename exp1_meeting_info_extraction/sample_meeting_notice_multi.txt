The 15th International Conference on Machine Learning and Applications (ICMLA 2026) will be held on December 12-15, 2026 in Sanya, China. The conference focuses on machine learning, deep learning and their real-world applications. Paper submission deadline is August 30, 2026. Registration fees are as follows: Early Bird (before Oct 1) 3800 CNY, Regular 4800 CNY, Student 2600 CNY, and On-site 5500 CNY. All fees are in CNY.
